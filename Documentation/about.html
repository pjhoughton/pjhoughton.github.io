👤 About Me

Hi, I’m Paul. I’ve worked in the IT industry for over 20 years and am currently a Senior Systems Engineer in a predominantly Windows and VMware environment. While I have extensive experience in enterprise systems, I started building my homelab to strengthen my skills in Linux, containers, and open‑source technologies. This journey allows me to explore Docker, hypervisors, HTML, and home automation — blending decades of professional expertise with modern, open‑source tools.

📖 Biography

My career has spanned infrastructure, virtualization, and systems engineering. With a strong background in Windows and VMware, I wanted to challenge myself by diving into areas I wasn’t as confident in — particularly Linux and containerization. My homelab is the perfect environment to experiment, learn, and document my progress while sharing knowledge with others.
